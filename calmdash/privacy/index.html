Calmdash Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application Calmdash ("Application"), made by TwentyFive Studios. The Application is a dashboard for your phone in the car. It can open when your car's Bluetooth connects and shows your speed, music controls, favourite contacts, Home and Work shortcuts, and an optional log of your drives.

Information Kept on Your Device

The Application keeps your settings, car names, favourites, Home and Work, and drives on your device. Uninstalling the Application removes them from your phone.

Location

Speed and stopping: If you allow location, Calmdash may use your phone's location while the dashboard is on screen to show your speed, and on screens where you type while your car is connected to tell whether you've stopped. It is used on your phone only. While your car is connected, Calmdash may also use your location and your phone's motion sensor to rest GPS and dim the screen when you've stopped, and to wake them when you drive off. This stays on your phone.

Day and night: If you allow location, Calmdash may use your approximate last known location on your phone to switch between its light and dark look at sunrise and sunset. It is used on your phone only.

Drive Log and Backup

If you turn on drive logging, Calmdash may use your phone's precise location while your car's Bluetooth is connected to record each drive: when it starts and ends, the route, distance and speed, and the places where drives start and end. Drives and the place names you give are saved on your phone. If your phone's Google backup is on, Android may include your drives and place names in its backup to your Google account. You can turn drive logging off or delete drives in the Application's settings. If the drive database is ever damaged, a copy may stay on your phone for up to 30 days; deleting all drives removes it. A mileage export is a file you choose to save. App statistics may record that a drive was logged, with rounded distance and time ranges, not where you went.

Home and Work

If you set Home or Work, Calmdash keeps the address, the spot or the place you choose on your phone, and gives it to Google Maps or Waze when you tap it. If you use where you are now, the Application may use your phone's location once, on your phone only.

Music Controls and Notification Access

If you turn on music controls, Calmdash may read which music app is playing and the song's title and artist from your phone's media notifications, to show them on the dashboard and control playback. This information stays on your phone. App statistics may record which kind of music app was used, not the song.

Favourites and Calls

If you add favourites, Calmdash keeps the name and number you pick in your phone's contact picker, on your phone. If you allow it, the Application may start a call when you tap a favourite, and may answer or decline a ringing call when you tap its buttons. When a call rings, the Application may show the caller's name from your phone's call notification; it is used on your phone only. App statistics may record which kind of phone app handled the call, not the caller.

Automatically Collected Information

Calmdash may collect app statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase to improve the Application. They don't include your contacts, songs, places or car names. These services may automatically collect certain information, including the type of mobile device you use, your device's unique identification ID, your IP address, your mobile operating system, and information about how you use the Application. Statistics may include how your car's Bluetooth connects (its make, if the stereo's name shows it, and connection timings), not its name, its address or where you are. Specifically, the Application uses:

Google Firebase Analytics: for understanding how the Application is used, such as which features are most popular. Analytics and crash reports use a random app identifier that is not linked to your name or contact details.

Google Firebase Crashlytics: for collecting crash reports when the Application encounters an error. This may include device state, device identifiers, and diagnostic information to help us fix issues.

Google Firebase Remote Config: for changing the Application's settings without an update.

You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data at policies.google.com/privacy.

In-App Purchases

If you buy the unlock, Google Play handles the payment. Calmdash keeps on your phone whether the unlock is active, and app statistics may record that a purchase happened. We do not directly handle or store payment information; your payment details are subject to Google's payment privacy policy.

Third-Party Data Sharing

The third-party services integrated into the Application (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Google Play Billing) may collect and process data as described above. Beyond those services, information may only be disclosed: as required by law or to comply with legal process; when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others; or if the Application is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it using the standard uninstall process available on your mobile device or via your app store. You can turn off location, notification access and drive logging in your phone's settings or the Application's settings at any time. For any opt-out requests, contact us at twentyfiveapps@outlook.com.

Data Retention

Data collected by the third-party services listed above is retained according to Google's data retention policies. If you would like to request deletion of any data associated with your use of the Application, please contact us at twentyfiveapps@outlook.com and we will respond in a reasonable time.

Children

We do not use the Application to knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided information through the Application without their consent, they should contact us at twentyfiveapps@outlook.com. We will take steps to remove such information within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the Application, but no system can prevent all security breaches.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this policy periodically, as continued use of the Application constitutes acceptance of any changes.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. "Processing" includes collecting, storing, deleting, using, combining, and disclosing information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us at twentyfiveapps@outlook.com.
